How to sculpt

Draw on the model with Apple Pencil. Fingers still turn, pinch and pan the view, and a stroke that starts off the model turns it too. Hover the Pencil to see the brush. Each stroke is one undo step.

With a mouse or trackpad, drag on the model to sculpt and drag off it to turn. [ and ] change the brush size.

    Individual fixes

    Merge duplicate points

    Joins vertices at the same spot and closes hairline seams.

    Fix flipped faces

    Points every face outward so the slicer knows inside from outside.

    Fill holes

    Caps open edges so the surface is closed.

    Remove loose bits

    Deletes small floating pieces, like stray triangles from an export.

    Turn inside out

    Flips every face. Use when the whole model reads as hollow.

    Cut flat 0.0 mm

    Slices the model at a height and caps the cut with a flat face. Trimming the bottom gives a curved model a stable base.

    Smooth 3 passes

    Softens faceted or noisy surfaces, such as scans. The base stays flat.

    Reduce triangles 50%

    Shrinks heavy files so slicers load them faster.
